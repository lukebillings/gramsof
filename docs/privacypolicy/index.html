Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Gramsof (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the Gramsof mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Gramsof is designed to keep your protein tracking data on your device. We may collect limited information needed to provide the service, such as:

  • Food log entries and protein goals you enter in the app
  • App preferences and settings stored on your device
  • Purchase and subscription status processed by Apple

How we use information

We use information solely to operate and improve Gramsof, including syncing your progress within the app, fulfilling subscriptions, and responding to support requests you send us.

Third-party services

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. Apple’s privacy practices apply to payment and account data processed through the App Store.

Data retention

Your tracking data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. If you contact us for support, we retain correspondence only as long as needed to resolve your request.

Children’s privacy

Gramsof is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
